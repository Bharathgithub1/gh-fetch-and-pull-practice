1. The Honest Woodcutter

A woodcutter was cutting a tree near a river when his axe slipped and fell into the water.

He sat down and cried. A fairy appeared and showed him a golden axe.

"Is this yours?" she asked.

"No," said the woodcutter.

She then showed him a silver axe.

"No, that's not mine either."

Finally, she brought out his old iron axe.

"Yes! That's mine!"

Because he was honest, the fairy gave him all three axes.

Moral: Honesty is rewarded.
