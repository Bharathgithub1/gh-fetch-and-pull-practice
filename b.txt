2. The Lion and the Mouse

A lion was sleeping when a little mouse ran across his paw.

The lion caught the mouse.

"Please let me go," begged the mouse. "One day I may help you."

The lion laughed but released him.

A few days later, the lion was trapped in a hunter's net. The mouse heard him roaring and quickly chewed through the ropes.

The lion was free.

Moral: Even the smallest friend can be helpful.
